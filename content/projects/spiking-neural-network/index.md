Implemented **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)** — biologically-inspired neural architectures — for image classification on CIFAR-10:

- **Architectures**: SNN VGG-16 and SNN ResNet-20
- **Framework**: SpikingJelly (built on PyTorch)
- **Focus**: Exploring energy-efficient neural computation
- **Applications**: Neuromorphic hardware and edge AI

Spiking Neural Networks offer a promising path toward brain-inspired AI with significantly lower energy consumption, making them ideal for deployment on specialized neuromorphic hardware.
