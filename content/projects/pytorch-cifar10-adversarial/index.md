A comprehensive PyTorch pipeline for training and evaluating CNN architectures on the CIFAR-10 benchmark:

- **Architectures**: ResNet-18, VGG-16, DenseNet-121, GoogLeNet
- **Clean Evaluation**: Standard test-set accuracy measurement
- **Adversarial Robustness**: Evaluation against benchmark adversarial attacks
- **Modular Design**: Easy to extend with new models and attacks

This project serves as a foundation for adversarial machine learning research, providing baseline models and reproducible evaluation protocols.
